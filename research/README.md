# Research program

The research surface tests whether GSE's claims improve software-agent outcomes and under which conditions they stop helping.

Current themes include root-owner effectiveness, dynamic versus fixed organization, evidence selection, context recovery, minimal-engineering discipline, and harness design.

Use [`BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md`](BENCHMARK_PROTOCOL.md) for comparative experiments and [`QUESTIONS.md`](QUESTIONS.md) for open hypotheses. Research material is non-normative until promoted into the specification through evidence and review.

Read a scan to find a research question, then use the benchmark protocol to design a comparison. To contribute a source or challenge a conclusion, follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
